# Security Policy

## Responsible Disclosure

We take the security of our project seriously. If you discover a vulnerability, please follow the responsible disclosure process outlined below.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report security issues to **security@example.com**.

Include the following details in your report:

- Type of vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce
- Affected versions (including environment details)
- Any proof of concept or relevant logs
- Preferred acknowledgment name (optional)

You should receive an initial acknowledgment within **24 hours**. A more detailed response will follow within **48 hours**. We will provide status updates every 7 days until the issue is resolved.

### What to Expect

- Triage and priority assignment
- Collaborative investigation
- Public credit (with your consent)
- CVE assignment when appropriate

## Supported Versions

This policy applies to the **latest major stable release** of the project. Reports against development branches are accepted but may take longer to address.

## Embargo Policy

We request that you **do not publicly disclose** any vulnerability until:

- **90 days** have passed since your report, or
- A fix has been released to users, whichever comes first.

This embargo allows us to develop, test, and deploy a patch without placing users at risk. We will work diligently to minimize this period.

## Scope

The policy covers the latest stable release as described above. Older versions and third-party dependencies are out of scope.

## Out of Scope

The following are not considered valid security reports:

- Third-party dependency vulnerabilities (report to respective maintainer)
- Theoretical attacks without a practical demonstration
- Self-XSS without additional exploitation
- Social engineering or physical attacks
- Issues requiring unrealistic user interaction

## Acknowledgments

We maintain a **Hall of Fame** for researchers who responsibly disclose vulnerabilities. If you would like to be listed, please indicate so in your report.
